FIELD MANUAL / START HERE

A map for learning security.

Cybersecurity is a set of different jobs and skills—not one trick or a guaranteed 60-day transformation. Use this guide to know what to study, what the game actually lets you practise, and what still needs deeper training.

YOUR FIRST SESSION / ABOUT 15 MINUTES

Follow one clear loop.

  1. Learn: open Day 1 in the Academy and read the short explanation.
  2. Investigate: open a Case and look at the fictional evidence before answering.
  3. Practise: open a simulation; read its task, use the controls, then check your work.
  4. Reflect: read the debrief, record one takeaway in Notes, and revisit anything you missed.

SKILL MAP

What cybersecurity includes

“Available today” means a bounded prototype exercise exists. Other areas are planned learning paths, not hidden or finished features.

01 / STARTING POINT

Safety, scope & digital habits

Authorization, privacy, threat and risk language, reporting, account hygiene, and how to keep training inside a fictional lab.

AVAILABLE TODAYStarter cases, password/MFA lessons, scam recognition.
02 / IDENTITY

Accounts & access control

Authentication, passkeys and MFA, sessions, recovery, least privilege, role checks, and safe handling of shared devices.

AVAILABLE TODAYAccount cases and the Permission Desk toy exercise.
03 / FOUNDATIONS

Networks & systems

IP addressing, DNS, routing, Wi‑Fi, TLS concepts, network boundaries, operating-system updates, permissions, and backups.

FOUNDATION ONLYPaper-route and configuration puzzles; no packet capture or device inspection yet.
04 / DATA

Privacy & information protection

Data minimization, classification, retention, consent, encryption concepts, secret handling, and what should never enter notes or logs.

AVAILABLE TODAYData Minimizer and fictional data-handling decisions.
05 / BUILD

Secure software & supply chain

Threat modeling, authorization, input handling, output encoding, secure configuration, dependency trust, integrity, and useful error handling.

INTRODUCTORY ONLYSecure Code Review is pattern spotting; there is no live code runner or vulnerable target.
06 / OPERATE

Cloud & platform security

Asset ownership, shared responsibility, identity roles, safe defaults, secrets, change review, system hardening, and recovery readiness.

PLANNED EXPANSIONCurrent configuration puzzles are toy examples, not a cloud lab.
07 / DETECT

Monitoring & alert triage

What logs can show, baselines, timestamps, source context, signal versus noise, evidence confidence, and when to escalate.

ONE SIMULATED BUNDLEAlert Queue and the fixed IR‑04 training terminal.
08 / RESPOND

Incident handling & investigation

Confirm scope and authority, preserve observable facts, build a timeline, communicate carefully, document uncertainty, and coordinate with owners.

TABLETOP LEVELRunbook and synthetic logs; no real forensic images or case management yet.
09 / RECOVER

Resilience, governance & roles

Restore tests, continuity, risk ownership, policies, professional boundaries, and choosing a focused path such as defense, secure development, operations, or investigation.

FOUNDATION LEVELRecovery Check and a learning record; career-depth assessment remains future work.

What do I type?

  1. Open the Practice Floor and scroll to “Read the sign-in trail.”
  2. Type help, then press Enter or tap Run command.
  3. Try ls, then grep MFA_DENIED auth.log.
  4. Read the result. Submit answer 198.51.100.23, then decision owner-led.
